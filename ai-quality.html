A practical review checklist

Before automating a judgment,
define the judgment.

01
Write a usable label guide.

Define the label, required evidence, exclusions and ambiguous cases.

02
Keep ambiguity visible.

Allow an unclear or needs-review state instead of forcing every case into a confident label.

03
Review disagreements.

Separate human-to-human label differences from automated-to-human differences.

04
Measure the costly miss.

Check false negatives and false positives separately. Overall agreement can hide the errors that matter.
