Real work / Reporting automation / TELUS Digital

Let the report build itself.

A weekly quality digest should cost judgment, not assembly. Automating the build keeps the team's attention on the findings and the coaching.

Context & my contribution

The same report, without the weekly rebuild.

The problem

Every week a program's quality report needs the same pieces: audit completion against target, score trends, an agent-level view and where coaching should focus. Assembled by hand, that formatting work repeats and every manual step is a chance to carry over the wrong number.

My role

In my quality leadership role at TELUS Digital, I set up and run an automated weekly quality report for one program. The workflow merges the audit data into a formatted digest and PDF and delivers it by email on a weekly cadence. I maintain the job and watch its run notifications.

The job runs under my account and I own its generated outputs. Onward sharing with program leadership has happened through my manager, not through me.

Approach

Automate the assembly. Keep the judgment.

Design the digest for decisions

The report leads with the weekly trend and audit completion, moves to the agent-level view, then names the strongest and weakest scoring sections and closes with concrete coaching actions. The reader gets a coaching agenda, not a wall of scores.

Watch the job like a system

Scheduled jobs fail quietly. Run and failure notifications route to my inbox, so a broken run is visible instead of silently skipped.

Keep a human on the message

The automation assembles; people still read, challenge and coach. A generated figure that looks wrong gets questioned the same way a manual one does.

Workflow: audit data → merge template → formatted digest and PDF → scheduled email → run monitoring.
What the evidence supports

A working automation, described at the level the evidence reaches.

The documented output is the automated weekly report email with its generated document and PDF, produced on a weekly cadence since early August 2026. My forwards of the report to my manager and the job notifications addressed to my account are in my mail. The evidence supports that I set up and operate this reporting workflow.

It does not establish that I designed every formula or template element alone, and it makes no business-outcome claim. A fair check would be whether the report still runs and whether its coaching focus changes week to week. That is a measurement plan, not a result claimed here.
